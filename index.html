I am a doctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems in the COMPASS research group under Dr. Sahar Abdelnabi. I am currently based in Tübingen, Germany, where I work on better understanding how large language models work and how to make them safer.

Before this, I completed the Erasmus Mundus Master's in Language and Communication Technology (EMLCT) at the Université de Lorraine and the Universität des Saarlandes. I received my Bachelor's degrees in Computational Linguistics and Psychology from Indiana University.
